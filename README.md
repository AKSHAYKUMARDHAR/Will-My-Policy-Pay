# Will My Policy Pay?

Health insurance fine print, explained before you need it. A free tool for Indian families: pick your
health policy or upload its PDF, and see the terms that decide what a claim pays (room-rent cap,
co-payment, sub-limits, waiting periods) in Hindi, Bengali or English, each quoted with its page from
the insurer's own document. A bill simulator then shows, line by line, what a hospital bill would pay.

- Product requirements: [docs/PRD.md](docs/PRD.md)
- Status: in build. The evaluation runs on real, public policy documents, frozen before any prompt is written.

Built with Claude Code, after my [UPI Triage Agent](https://github.com/AKSHAYKUMARDHAR/UPI-Triage-Agent)
and [Is This a Scam?](https://github.com/AKSHAYKUMARDHAR/Is-This-A-Scam).
